## The seven grounded candidates (ordered by resonance)

Mechanism: a self-updating canonical catalogue for a Nairobi collective. Scene: a phone in a matatu, a WhatsApp link, 4G that drops. Cultural home: Nairobi's own graphic and screen traditions. Ruts kept out: the incumbent (near-black ground, orange→purple→blue gradient text, glass cards) and its opposite (cream paper, serif display, terracotta accent); anything a non-Kenyan would pick as "African".

1. **Matatu / nganya livery & route boards** — vehicle painting. The scene itself; airbrushed panels, route boards, LED strips, the sound system.
2. **Kanga / leso with the *jina* proverb strip** — textile print. Border + field + message line; every release a kanga.
3. **Hand-painted duka / kinyozi signage** — sign painting. Enamel on corrugated iron, numbered portrait grid, offset-shadow lettering. *(assigned by the roll)*
4. **Gengetone flyer & Kenyan cover-art tradition** — digital poster. Condensed caps, cut-out heads, a date strip.
5. **M-Pesa / USSD feature-phone grammar** — screen interface. Numbered menus, confirmation SMS blocks; the most-used interface in Kenya.
6. **Kenyan broadsheet / Nairobi magazine layout** — print editorial. Masthead and columns for the journal.
7. **Benga-era Kenyan sleeves & River Road cassette inlays** — record packaging. Two-colour offset, framed band photo, catalogue number.
